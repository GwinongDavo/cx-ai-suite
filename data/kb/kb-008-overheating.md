# Overheating, fan noise, and thermal throttling
Applies to: Aurora Book Pro 16, Aurora Edge X1

## Expected behaviour
The fans speed up during video calls, gaming, or compiling, and the bottom of the laptop can reach 45°C under load. This is within specification.

## Steps
1. In Aurora Vantage > Power, set the thermal mode to "Balanced" or "Quiet".
2. Make sure the vents on the bottom and rear are not blocked by soft surfaces like beds or cushions.
3. Check Task Manager for processes using more than 50% CPU while idle.
4. Update the BIOS and embedded controller firmware (KB-007), which contain fan curve fixes.

## When it is a fault
If the laptop shuts down from heat during light use, or the fan makes a grinding or clicking noise, the fan or thermal paste needs service under warranty.
