# Docking station and USB-C external display issues
Applies to: Aurora Dock U3, all Aurora laptops with USB-C or Thunderbolt

## No signal on external monitor
1. Update the dock firmware and the laptop's graphics and Thunderbolt drivers through Aurora Vantage.
2. Connect the dock to the laptop port marked with the Thunderbolt or DisplayPort icon. Other USB-C ports do not carry video.
3. The Aurora Dock U3 supports up to two 4K monitors at 60 Hz; a third monitor will not display.

## Dock not charging the laptop
The Aurora Dock U3 supplies 90W. The Aurora Book Pro 16 needs 100W under load, so it can discharge slowly during heavy use while docked; this is expected.

## Replacement
A dock that is not detected on multiple laptops after a firmware update is faulty and can be replaced as a parts-only unit (KB-012).
