# Battery not charging or charging slowly
Applies to: Aurora Book 14, Aurora Book Pro 16, Aurora Edge X1

## Symptoms
The battery icon shows "plugged in, not charging", the charge level stays the same while connected, or a full charge takes more than 4 hours. A battery that stops charging at 80% is usually not a fault: Battery Conservation Mode is enabled.

## Quick checks
1. Confirm the charger is the original 65W USB-C adapter (Aurora Book 14, Edge X1) or 100W adapter (Book Pro 16). Phone chargers under 45W charge very slowly or not at all.
2. Try a different wall socket and remove any extension lead.
3. Open Aurora Vantage > Power and turn off Battery Conservation Mode if the customer wants charging to 100%.
4. Check the charging LED next to the USB-C port: solid white means charging, blinking amber means a charging fault.

## Battery reset
Shut the laptop down, unplug the charger, then press and hold the power button for 15 seconds. Reconnect the charger and power on. This clears the embedded controller and fixes most "not charging" cases after a firmware update.

## When to replace the battery
Run the Battery Health test in Aurora Vantage. If health is below 60% of design capacity, or the battery health status reads "Replace", the battery is eligible for replacement under warranty during the first 12 months. After 12 months the battery is a consumable part and replacement is chargeable unless the customer has Premium Care.

## Safety: swollen battery
If the case is lifting, the trackpad is pushed up, or there is a hissing sound or burning smell, tell the customer to stop using the device and unplug it immediately. A swollen battery is a safety incident: escalate as P1 and arrange collection regardless of warranty status. Never ask the customer to remove a swollen battery themselves.
