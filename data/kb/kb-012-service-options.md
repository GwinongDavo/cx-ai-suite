# Repair service options and turnaround times
Applies to: all Aurora devices under warranty

## Depot repair
The customer receives a prepaid shipping box. Target turnaround is 5 to 7 business days from when the depot receives the device. Customers should back up their data first (KB-013).

## Onsite repair
Available with Premium Care and Premier Support. A technician visits the customer's address. Premier Support targets next business day onsite service when the case is logged before 3 pm local time.

## Parts-only (CRU)
Customer-replaceable units such as chargers, docks, and keyboards on the Aurora Edge X1 can be shipped directly to the customer, normally within 2 business days.

## Repair status
Customers can track a repair using the case number on the Aurora support website. Agents should share the case number in every reply.
