# Factory reset and system recovery
Applies to: all Aurora laptops running Windows 11

## Reset from Windows
Go to Settings > System > Recovery > Reset this PC. "Keep my files" removes apps and settings but keeps personal files. "Remove everything" erases the drive and is the right choice before selling or returning a device.

## Reset when Windows does not start
Interrupt startup three times to open Windows Recovery Environment, then choose Troubleshoot > Reset this PC. Aurora laptops also have a recovery partition, launched by pressing F11 at startup.

## Recovery USB
If the recovery partition is missing, the customer can create a recovery USB with the Aurora Recovery Media tool using the device serial number. A USB drive of at least 32 GB is needed.
