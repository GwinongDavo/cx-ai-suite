# Case priority, SLA targets, and escalation policy
Applies to: all support cases (internal agent guidance)

## Priority definitions
- P1 Critical: safety risk (swollen battery, smoke, burning smell), or a business customer with multiple devices down.
- P2 High: a single device unusable with customer data at risk, or a Premier Support customer with a device down.
- P3 Normal: the device works but has a fault (Wi-Fi drops, flicker, overheating).
- P4 Low: how-to questions, order status, and general information.

## SLA targets
First response: P1 within 1 hour, P2 within 4 hours, P3 within 8 hours, P4 within 24 hours.
Resolution: P1 within 24 hours, P2 within 48 hours, P3 within 5 days, P4 within 7 days.
Premier Support cases use half of these resolution targets.

## When to escalate to Level 2
Escalate when troubleshooting in the knowledge base has been completed without success, when the customer has contacted support three or more times about the same issue, when there is a safety risk, or when the customer asks for a refund or credit outside policy. Legal threats and media complaints go to the escalations manager, not Level 2.

## Handoff note
Every escalation must include the serial number, warranty status, steps already tried, and the customer's preferred contact method, so the customer does not have to repeat themselves.
