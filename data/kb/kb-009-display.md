# Display flicker, lines, and dead pixels
Applies to: all Aurora laptops

## Flicker and lines
Flicker that happens only in some apps is usually a graphics driver issue: update or roll back the graphics driver. Flicker or lines that also appear in the BIOS screen (press F2 at startup) point to a hardware fault in the panel or display cable.

## Dead pixel policy
A display is eligible for replacement under warranty if it has 3 or more bright pixels, 5 or more dark pixels, or any bright pixel in the central area of the screen. Fewer defects than this are within the panel specification and are not replaced.

## Physical damage
Cracked screens or pressure marks are physical damage and are only covered with Accidental Damage Protection (KB-003).
