# Checking warranty status and coverage
Applies to: all Aurora devices

## How to find the serial number
The serial number is on the label under the device, in Aurora Vantage > Device, or by running `wmic bios get serialnumber` in Command Prompt. Aurora serial numbers are 10 characters, starting with two letters followed by eight digits or letters, for example PF3A9K2L01.

## Warranty tiers
- Base Warranty: 12 months parts and labour, depot (mail-in) repair.
- Premium Care: 24 or 36 months, onsite or depot repair, 24/7 phone support, battery covered for the full term.
- Premier Support (business only): 36 to 60 months, next business day onsite repair, a dedicated technical account manager, and priority routing for all cases.

## What warranty does not cover
Physical damage, liquid damage, cosmetic wear, and software issues caused by third-party applications are not covered by Base Warranty. Physical and liquid damage are only covered if the customer bought Accidental Damage Protection (see KB-003).

## Expired warranty
If the warranty has expired, the customer can still get a paid repair quote or buy a warranty extension within 30 days of expiry without an inspection. After 30 days, an extension requires a remote diagnostic first. Agents must not offer free repairs on expired devices without supervisor approval.
