# Keyboard or trackpad not working
Applies to: all Aurora laptops

## Steps
1. Check that the trackpad is not disabled with the F6 trackpad toggle key.
2. Restart in BIOS (F2). If the keyboard works in BIOS, the problem is a Windows driver; reinstall it from Aurora Vantage.
3. For sticky keys after a spill, follow the liquid damage advice in KB-003.

## Raised trackpad
A trackpad that is raised, stiff, or does not click can be a sign of a swollen battery underneath. Follow the swollen battery safety steps in KB-001 and escalate as P1.
