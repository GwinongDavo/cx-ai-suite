# Data backup and privacy before repair
Applies to: all repairs

## Backup
Repairs can involve replacing or reimaging the storage drive. Ask customers to back up their files before sending a device for repair. Aurora is not responsible for data on devices sent for repair.

## Keep your drive
Premier Support customers can choose the "Keep Your Drive" option: the technician replaces a failed drive and the customer keeps the old one, for data security.

## Identity verification
Before discussing case details, changing a shipping address, or sharing repair information, verify the customer's identity with the email on the account plus the device serial number. Never ask for a customer's Windows password or full payment card number.
