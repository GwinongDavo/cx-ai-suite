# Returns and refunds
Applies to: devices bought directly from the Aurora online store

## Return window
Customers can return a device within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it is in its original condition with all accessories. Devices bought from retailers must be returned to that retailer.

## Restocking and exceptions
There is no restocking fee for unopened devices. Opened devices with a custom configuration (CTO) carry a 15% restocking fee. Devices with physical damage cannot be returned.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days after the returned device is inspected at the warehouse.

## Agent authority
Agents can approve standard returns inside the 30-day window. Refunds or goodwill credits outside the policy need supervisor approval; agents must not promise them to the customer.
