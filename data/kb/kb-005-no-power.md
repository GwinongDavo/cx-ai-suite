# Laptop does not power on
Applies to: all Aurora laptops

## First checks
Confirm the charger LED is lit and the charging LED on the laptop turns on when the charger is connected. Disconnect all USB devices, docks, and SD cards.

## Hard reset
Unplug the charger, press and hold the power button for 30 seconds, plug the charger back in, wait 5 minutes, and press the power button once. On the Aurora Edge X1, there is also an emergency reset pinhole on the bottom panel: press it for 10 seconds with a paperclip.

## Signs of a display-only fault
If the keyboard backlight turns on, fans spin, or the Caps Lock key toggles its light, the laptop is powering on but the display is not working. Ask the customer to connect an external monitor. If the external monitor works, treat it as a display issue (KB-009).

## Next step
If nothing responds after a hard reset with a known-good charger, the device needs a hardware repair. Check warranty status (KB-002) and book onsite or depot service (KB-012).
