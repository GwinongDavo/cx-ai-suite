# Accidental Damage Protection (ADP) claims
Applies to: devices with an active ADP add-on

## What ADP covers
ADP covers drops, spills, electrical surges, and cracked screens from accidental use. It does not cover theft, loss, intentional damage, or cosmetic damage that does not affect function.

## Claim limits
Consumers get one ADP claim per 12-month period. Business customers with Premier Support get two claims per 12-month period. There is no excess fee for ADP repairs.

## Starting a claim
Ask the customer for the serial number, a short description of how the damage happened, the date it happened, and photos of the damage. Claims must be opened within 30 days of the incident. Liquid damage: tell the customer to power the device off, unplug it, and not try to dry it with a hair dryer.

## Turnaround
ADP repairs are handled as depot repairs for consumers, with a target of 7 business days from receipt. Premier Support customers get onsite ADP repairs.
