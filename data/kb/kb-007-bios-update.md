# Updating BIOS and firmware safely
Applies to: all Aurora devices

## Before updating
The laptop must be connected to its charger and the battery must be above 30%. Suspend BitLocker first (Control Panel > BitLocker > Suspend protection) or the customer will be asked for the BitLocker recovery key after the update.

## How to update
Use Aurora Vantage > Updates, which installs BIOS, embedded controller, and Thunderbolt firmware in the right order. Do not turn off the device during the update. The screen can go black for up to 5 minutes; this is normal.

## If the update fails
If the device does not start after a failed BIOS update, do not attempt repeated power cycles. Escalate to Level 2: Aurora laptops have a BIOS recovery mode that a technician will guide the customer through.

## BitLocker recovery key
If the customer is asked for a BitLocker recovery key, they can find it at their Microsoft account under Devices > Manage recovery keys, or from their company IT department for business devices. Aurora support cannot retrieve or bypass BitLocker keys.
